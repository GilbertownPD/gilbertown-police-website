Immediate danger or urgent help

Emergency: Call 911

Call 911 when there is an immediate threat to life, safety, or property, or when urgent police, fire, or medical response is needed.

  • A crime is in progress or someone is attempting to commit a crime.
  • Violence, threats, weapons, or a dangerous disturbance is occurring.
  • There is a fire, smoke, gas odor, serious injury, or medical emergency.
  • A crash has injuries, blocked traffic, or another urgent road hazard.
Call 911

Police assistance without immediate danger

Non-Emergency Dispatch

Contact Choctaw County E-911 Dispatch when police assistance or a dispatch-related response is needed, but there is no immediate emergency or crime actively occurring.

  • Reporting a non-urgent concern or suspicious activity.
  • Requesting police assistance for a situation that is not an emergency.
  • Reporting an incident discovered after the fact.
  • Seeking dispatch-related assistance when no immediate danger exists.
Choctaw County E-911: (205) 459-5911

Forms, records, and general information

Police Department Office

Contact the Gilbertown Police Department office for administrative questions, forms, records information, department information, and other non-dispatch office inquiries.

  • Questions about department forms or request procedures.
  • Records or report request information.
  • Vacation Watch, complaint, or commendation form questions.
  • General office information and public-safety resources.
Gilbertown PD Office: (251) 843-2766

Not sure if it is an emergency? Call 911 and explain what is happening. The dispatcher can determine the appropriate response.
