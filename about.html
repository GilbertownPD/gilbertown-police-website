Gilbertown Police Department

Focused on public safety and community service

The Gilbertown Police Department serves the Town of Gilbertown, Alabama, by providing professional law-enforcement service, supporting public safety, and connecting residents with appropriate local and county resources.

This website provides access to department information, public forms, safety materials, community notices, and helpful links. The Submit a Tip page is available now for non-emergency information.

Residents can use this website as a public information resource and contact the appropriate agency or service when assistance is needed.

Our Purpose

Service with professionalism and respect

The department is committed to serving the Gilbertown community with professionalism, fairness, integrity, respect, and a continued focus on public safety.

To serve and protect the people of Gilbertown through lawful, fair, and professional police service.

Important Contact Guidance

Know who to contact

Call 911 for emergencies, crimes in progress, immediate danger, fire, medical emergencies, serious injuries, or urgent threats to people or property.

Contact Choctaw County E-911 Dispatch for non-emergency police dispatch assistance. Contact the Gilbertown Police Department office for forms, records questions, general department information, and other office inquiries.

If you are unsure whether a situation is an emergency, call 911 and explain what is happening. The dispatcher can determine the appropriate response.
