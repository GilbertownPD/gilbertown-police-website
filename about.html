About the Department

The Gilbertown Police Department serves Gilbertown, Alabama. The department works to protect life and property, preserve the peace, enforce the law fairly, and maintain a safe community for residents and visitors.

Mission

To serve and protect the people of Gilbertown through lawful, fair, and professional police service.

Our Commitment

The Gilbertown Police Department is committed to professionalism, fairness, integrity, respect, accountability, and clear communication with the public.

Department Leadership

Chief of Police: Chief Frank Burkett
