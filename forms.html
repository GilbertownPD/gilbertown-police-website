Before You Submit

A few helpful reminders

Use a current PDF reader to complete and save fillable forms. Keep a copy of every form and any supporting information you provide for your own records.

If you need help selecting a form, need an accommodation, or need submission assistance, use the department’s Contact page before submitting your request.

Step 1

Download

Open the regular PDF to view or print a blank form, or use the fillable version to type information electronically.

Step 2

Complete

Provide complete, accurate information and include any documents or details requested by the form.

Step 3

Save & Submit

Save the completed file, then follow the directions on the form or contact the department for an approved submission method.

Step 4

Keep Records

Keep your submission date, copies, receipts, confirmation numbers, and relevant contact information.

Important: Do not use this website, a form, or email to report an emergency, a crime in progress, or a situation requiring immediate police response. Call 911 for emergencies.
