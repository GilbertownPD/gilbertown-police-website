Available Forms

Download, print, and complete the appropriate form. Select Fillable PDF to complete a form with a keyboard and save a copy to your device, or select Print PDF to print a blank form for handwriting.

Forms are not monitored for emergency reporting. For an emergency or crime in progress, call 911.

Submitting Most Forms

Unless a form states otherwise, completed forms may be delivered to the Chief of Police in person, left with the Town Clerk for delivery to the Chief, mailed to 40 Front Street, Gilbertown, Alabama 36908, or emailed to gilbertownchief@outlook.com.

Complaint Forms: Must be delivered in person by the complainant with an original signature. Complaint Forms may not be submitted by email or through this website.

Public Records Request Form

Use this form to request public records from the Gilbertown Police Department. Requests are reviewed before records are released.

No processing fees are charged at this time. Release of records remains subject to applicable law.

Complaint Form

Use this form to submit a formal complaint for department review. All fields must be completed by the complainant. Anonymous complaints are not accepted through this form.

In-person submission required: The complainant must personally deliver the completed Complaint Form to the Chief of Police with an original signature. If the Chief is unavailable, the form may be left and the Chief will contact the complainant at his convenience. Do not email this form.

The Fillable PDF may be completed on a computer for convenience, but it must be printed, signed in original ink, and personally delivered by the complainant to the Chief of Police.

Print PDF Fillable PDF

Vacation Watch Request Form

Use this form to request vacation-watch attention for a residence within the Gilbertown Police Department service area.

Submit the completed form before leaving town whenever possible, and notify the department when you return so the request can be closed.
