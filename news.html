Community Reminder

Know the Right Contact Number

Call 911 for emergencies, crimes in progress, immediate danger, fire, serious injury, medical emergencies, or urgent threats. For non-emergency police dispatch assistance, contact Choctaw County E-911 Dispatch at (205) 459-5911.

For office questions, forms, records information, and general department inquiries, call the Gilbertown Police Department office at (251) 843-2766.
