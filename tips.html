Submit a Tip

Share information that may help the Gilbertown Police Department. Do not use this form for emergencies, crimes in progress, or situations requiring immediate police response.

Non-Emergency Information Only

Your information may help

Tips can provide useful information about non-emergency concerns, suspicious activity, past incidents, possible criminal activity, or other matters that may assist the department.

Share facts you know or observed. Include dates, times, locations, descriptions, vehicle information, usernames, links, or other details when they are available and safe to provide.

Do not put yourself at risk to gather information. Do not confront, follow, or attempt to investigate a person or situation yourself.

Emergency or Crime in Progress?

Contact options

Have photos, video, or screenshots? This form cannot accept attachments. Describe what you have and leave a phone number or email above so an officer can follow up to collect it.

Do not include passwords, Social Security numbers, bank account information, or other unnecessary sensitive personal information. Do not use this form for emergencies.


Before You Submit

Helpful information to include

  • What happened or what behavior caused concern.
  • Date, time, and exact or approximate location.
  • Descriptions of people, clothing, vehicles, plates, or direction of travel.
  • Names, usernames, profile links, web addresses, or platform names when relevant.
  • Whether you have photos, screenshots, video, messages, or other evidence, and how we can reach you to collect it.

For a non-emergency police-dispatch need, contact Choctaw County E-911 Dispatch at (205) 459-5911.
