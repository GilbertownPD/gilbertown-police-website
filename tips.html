Submit a Tip

The Gilbertown Police Department welcomes information that may assist law enforcement or public safety.

Do not use this page to report an emergency, a crime in progress, or a situation needing an immediate response. Call 911 for emergencies. For non-emergency dispatch, contact Choctaw County E-911 Dispatch at (205) 459-5911.

Tip Submission Under Development

A web-based tip option is under development. This page will be updated when submission procedures, monitoring practices, and privacy information are established.

Do not send emergency information, evidence, photographs, video, passwords, or other sensitive files through an unapproved web submission process.
