Community Connection

Public-safety outreach and local events

This page will include Gilbertown Police Department-posted community events, public-safety outreach activities, meetings, reminders, and other approved opportunities for community engagement.

Event information will be added when it is confirmed and approved for publication. Dates, times, locations, accessibility information, and contact details will be included with each posted event.

Emergency Reminder

Events are not emergency services

Do not wait for an event, meeting, website update, or social-media post if immediate help is needed. Call 911 for emergencies, crimes in progress, fire, medical emergencies, or immediate danger.
