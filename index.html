A Message From the Chief

Welcome to the Gilbertown Police Department

Our goal is to provide our community with clear, reliable information about the department, available services, public notices, and ways to contact us. We are committed to serving the Town of Gilbertown with professionalism, fairness, integrity, and respect.

This website will continue to grow as we add approved public forms, community resources, safety information, and department updates. I encourage residents to use this site as a source of information and to contact the department when we can be of assistance.

To serve and protect the people of Gilbertown through lawful, fair, and professional police service.

Chief Frank BurkettGilbertown Police Department
