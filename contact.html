Contact Guidance

Choose the right contact option

For immediate danger, crimes in progress, fire, medical emergencies, or urgent threats to people or property, call 911. Do not use a website, online form, email, social-media message, or tip submission when immediate police, fire, or medical help is needed.

For non-emergency police dispatch assistance, contact Choctaw County E-911 Dispatch. For office questions, forms, records, administrative inquiries, and general department information, contact the Gilbertown Police Department office.
