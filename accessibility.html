Website Accessibility

Accessibility

How the Gilbertown Police Department works to make this website usable for everyone, and how to get help.

Our Commitment

A website everyone can use

The Gilbertown Police Department wants every resident and visitor, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website. We work to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, and we review the site as pages are added or changed.

What this website does

  • Works on phones, tablets, and computers, and text can be enlarged with your browser's zoom.
  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, and the menu closes with the Escape key.
  • Form fields, buttons, and images have labels or descriptions for screen readers.
  • Phone numbers can be tapped to call from a mobile phone.
  • Text and background colors are chosen for strong contrast.
  • Moving elements, such as the home page slideshow, slow down or stop when your device is set to reduce motion.

Known limitations

  • Some PDF forms and safety guides may not work fully with screen readers. If you need a document in another format, contact the department and we will work with you.
  • Links to outside websites go to pages the department does not control, and those sites may not meet the same standards.

Report a problem or ask for help

If you have trouble using any part of this website, please let us know. Tell us which page you were on, what the problem was, and the best way to reach you. You can call the department office at (251) 843-2766 or email gilbertownchief@outlook.com.
