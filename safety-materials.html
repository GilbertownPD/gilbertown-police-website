Community Safety & Preparedness

Safety Materials

Free, printable guides and worksheets to help individuals and families prepare for emergencies, prevent crime and fraud, protect personal information, and respond safely when something happens.

Preparedness Starts at Home

Practical resources for everyday safety

These materials are designed to be printed, completed, and kept where they are useful—such as a glove box, emergency kit, family computer area, home office, pet carrier, or secure document file.

Review and update personal information whenever it changes. Share completed materials with the people in your household who may need them during an emergency or unexpected situation.

Important Reminder

Emergency? Call 911.

These guides are for planning and reference. They are not a substitute for emergency services, immediate police response, fire response, medical care, or instructions from local officials.

Keep the right guide in the right place

Complete the write-in areas with your household, make copies for people who need them, and review the materials at least once each year or after a move, emergency, new vehicle, new pet, medication change, insurance change, or other major life event.
