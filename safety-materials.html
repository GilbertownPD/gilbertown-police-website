Safety Materials

Free, printable guides and worksheets to help households prepare for emergencies, prevent crime and fraud, protect personal information, and respond safely when something happens.

Practical tools for everyday safety

These materials are designed for printing, sharing with family members, and keeping where they are useful—such as a go-bag, glove box, home office, family computer area, or secure document file. Review and update personal information whenever it changes.

Emergency? Call 911.

These guides are for planning and reference. They are not a substitute for emergency services, instructions from local officials, or immediate police, fire, or medical response.

Make the materials work for your household

Complete the write-in areas together, make copies for the people who need them, and review them at least once a year or after a major life change, move, new vehicle, new pet, medication change, or emergency event.

  • Glove box Traffic-crash checklist and driver-information card.
  • Go-bag or emergency kit Household emergency plan and essential contacts.
  • Secure document storage Property inventory, receipts, insurance information, and copies of records.
  • Shared family space Online-safety guide, scam guide, and home/vacation checklists.
